GrowDesk Capture
for WhatsApp Web and Instagram

Capture leads from conversations straight into GrowDesk. Connect this PC once, then press START on the toolbar.

Connect to GrowDesk

Your admin creates a connection for this PC in GrowDesk under Administration → Capture Tool → Connections.

The address you open GrowDesk at.

This address uses plain http, so the connection is not encrypted: anyone on the same network could read the secret and the leads you send. Fine for getting started; move GrowDesk to https when it has a domain.

How to capture a lead

▶ Watch the animated guide in GrowDesk. The ? on the toolbar opens it too.

  1. Open WhatsApp Web or Instagram. The GrowDesk toolbar appears at the top.
  2. Press START.
  3. Highlight a name or number, right-click, then choose GrowDesk Capture → Set as …. Pick treatments and other lists from the toolbar.
  4. Press STOP. GrowDesk adds the customer, or updates them if they already exist.
